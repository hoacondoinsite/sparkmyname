Privacy Policy

SparkMyName uses your information to create and deliver your brand, process your payment, give you access to your Command Center, and support your account. SparkMyName does not sell your personal information.

Last updated: June 29, 2026

1. What we collect

  • Your email address — to deliver your package and send you a sign-in link.
  • Your order information — what you purchased and when.
  • The business description you enter — so we can create names that fit it.
  • Payment information — handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic usage data — standard technical information your browser sends, used to keep the service working.
  • Your acknowledgment record — the first time you download or use an asset, we record the name you type, your account email, the date and time, and the version of the terms you accepted, so both you and we have a record that you reviewed and agreed to them before use.
  • Assets you create or download — the brands, designs, and documents created for you, kept so you can return to them.

    • 2. How we use it

    We use it to create and deliver your Brand Kit, process your payment, let you access your purchased brands, provide support, and improve the service. We also keep a record of the terms you accepted before downloading, so we can show what you agreed to if a question ever arises. We do not sell your personal information.

    3. Companies that help us run the service

    To run SparkMyName we use trusted third parties, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Stripe — secure payment processing.
  • Supabase — secure storage of your account and packages, and passwordless sign-in.
  • Resend — delivery of your package and sign-in emails.
  • Netlify — secure hosting and delivery of the website.
  • OpenAI — the business description you enter is sent to create your names. Don’t include sensitive personal information in that description.

    • 4. Cookies and what your browser saves

    We use minimal cookies and browser storage — mainly to keep you signed in and to carry your request through checkout. We do not use them for advertising.

    5. How long we keep your info

    In plain words: we keep your brands so you can come back to them any time, and we keep a record of the terms you agreed to.

    We keep your account and the brands and assets you’ve created so you can return and access them. We also retain your acknowledgment records — the terms you accepted, with your name, email, date, and version — as part of our business and legal records, and we may keep these even after an account is deleted, to the extent needed to establish what was agreed to or to comply with law. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Section 6).

    6. Your rights and choices

    In plain words: it’s your info. Email us and we can show it to you, fix it, or delete it.

    You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing hello@sparkmyname.com . We’ll respond within a reasonable time. Note that we may retain certain limited records — such as the terms you accepted before downloading — where we have a legal or legitimate business reason to keep them.

    7. How we protect it

    We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including passwordless “magic-link” sign-in so there is no password to be stolen. No method of transmission or storage is ever 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

    8. Adults only

    SparkMyName is intended for adults building businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. Please do not use the service or provide information if you are under 18.

    9. Updates, and how to reach us

    We may update this policy from time to time; the “last updated” date reflects the current version. Questions about your privacy? Email hello@sparkmyname.com .
