New U.S. Patent Pending platform brings naming, logos, brand identity, visuals, messaging, and launch assets together in one experience

FLORIDA — 2026 — SparkMyName today announced the public launch of its Brand Intelligence Platform, a new all-in-one approach to helping entrepreneurs, creators, professionals, and organizations build complete, launch-ready brands.

While many naming tools stop at creating lists of possible business names, SparkMyName was built to solve a much bigger problem: turning an idea into a real brand people can recognize, trust, and launch.

SparkMyName combines intelligent naming with full brand development in one organized experience. Depending on the project, users may receive brand naming, logo systems, color and typography direction, brand voice, launch messaging, cinematic brand imagery, social-ready assets, domain guidance, and a private online Brand Home where everything is organized and ready to use.

The platform was designed around a simple belief: founders do not just need ideas — they need a brand system they can actually use in the real world.

“The hard part was never just having an idea,” said founder Peter Klein. “The hard part was turning that idea into something real — something clear, memorable, professional, and ready to launch. We built SparkMyName to give people more than suggestions. We built it to give them a brand they can actually use.”

Unlike traditional naming tools that rely primarily on keywords, SparkMyName applies a structured Brand Intelligence framework designed to understand that every industry communicates differently. A law firm, restaurant, creator brand, nonprofit, startup, contractor, beauty business, or professional service each needs a different kind of identity, language, and visual direction. SparkMyName was built around that principle.

The platform is designed to help customers move from concept to execution with more clarity, speed, and confidence. By bringing the key parts of branding into one place, SparkMyName helps users avoid the fragmented process of hiring separate services for naming, visuals, messaging, and launch support.

Brand packages may include:

  • Brandable business names
  • Available domain recommendations
  • Ready-to-use logos, including primary, reverse, and brand mark versions
  • Color palette and typography direction
  • Brand voice and positioning guidance
  • Taglines, bios, and launch messaging
  • Social-handle suggestions and profile-ready assets
  • Cinematic brand imagery
  • Organized delivery inside a private Brand Home
  • Practical next steps for launching the brand

    • SparkMyName serves customers across industries and is built for a global audience of founders, startups, creators, agencies, professionals, nonprofits, and businesses developing new brands, products, and ventures.
