Cookie Policy

SparkMyName uses minimal cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, carry your request through checkout, and help the site work properly. Spark does not use cookies for advertising.

SparkMyName uses only what’s needed to run the service. We do not use advertising cookies, marketing pixels, or third-party tracking — and nothing here ever adds a charge.

What we use, and why

Secure checkout (Stripe). When you pay, Stripe sets cookies needed to process the payment securely and prevent fraud.

Sign-in (Supabase). When you log in to your Brand Home, a session is kept so you stay signed in.

Your device storage. We use your browser’s local storage to remember things like your email and drafts on your own device. This stays on your device and is not a tracking cookie.

What we don’t do

  • No Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or ad networks.
  • No selling or sharing of your activity for advertising.
  • No non-essential trackers — so there’s no consent banner to click through.

    • Turning cookies off

    You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may prevent checkout or sign-in from working. Clearing your browser’s local storage will remove saved drafts on that device.
