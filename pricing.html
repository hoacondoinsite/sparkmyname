Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

Is it really just one payment?
Yes. $99, one time. No subscription, no trial that bills you later, no hidden costs. What you build is yours to keep and use anywhere.
Do I own everything?
Yes — your brand and its files are yours, royalty-free, to use however you like. You own your brand by registering the domain and securing your rights.
What if I don’t love the names?
You get six complete brands to choose from, not one. If none feel right, you can ask for another full set of six — we build them and email you when they’re ready.
How fast do I get it?
Your core brand kit lands in your workspace within minutes. Catalog items you order — cards, signage, apparel — are custom-made and delivered within 24 hours, with an email when they’re ready.
Can you build my website for me?
We give you a website starter pack — a ready-to-use template themed to your brand — plus a step-by-step guide. You upload it to a host you choose (like Hostinger or GoDaddy). SparkMyName does not build or host your live site for you.
Are the social handles guaranteed available?
We recommend handles that fit your brand and give you one-tap links to check them. Availability changes constantly, so confirm each one on the platform before you claim it.
Do you clear trademarks?
No. SparkMyName does not perform trademark clearance or provide legal advice. Always run your own trademark check before using any name.
