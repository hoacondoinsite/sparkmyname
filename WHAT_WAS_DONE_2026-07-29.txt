WHY A PLAYER AND NOT A <video src>: a film is not one file. Each film is several scene
  .mp4s plus ONE continuous narration .mp3 and ONE music .mp3 (proven in
  premiere-produce-background.js: scenes upload as slug-sceneN.mp4, voice as slug-voice.mp3,
  music as slug-music.mp3; the manifest records {voice, music, shots[]} per brand).

DUPLICATES — HANDLED WITHOUT GUESSING: storage holds orphan files from earlier versions
  (odyssey1-4, luxheli, marquee, sparkstudios). The player never reads storage directly; it
  reads the manifest, and the producer PRUNES the manifest to the current BRANDS list
  ("prune keys for brands no longer on the wall"). Orphans are therefore unreachable by
  construction — no filename guessing anywhere in the player.

NEW FILES:
  css/film-player.css  — modal, stage, bottom control line, captions, reduced-motion support.
  js/film-player.js    — the player. window.SMNFilm.open(slug,{name,cat,source,opener}).
  js/film-captions.js  — narration text for ADA captions, generated from the EXACT `script`
                         string each producer sends to the TTS engine (11 films). The words
                         a caption shows are the words the narrator speaks. TIMING IS
                         PROPORTIONAL (cues spread by character count), not hand-authored —
                         wording exact, cue boundaries approximate. Stated in the file header.

HOW IT PLAYS: two alternating <video> elements. While scene N plays, scene N+1 is already
  loading on the other element, so the hand-off has nothing to wait for (the Founder's
  "fast buffering" preserved). Narration and music run as one unbroken track underneath and
  are re-synced to the film clock whenever drift exceeds 0.35s. One scrubber spans the whole
  film: every scene's real duration is measured on open, so seeking to 30% of a 3-scene film
  lands in the correct scene at the correct second.

ZIP 14 — 0140 — REALTIME VOICE INTAKE (flagship model, rolling sessions)
====================================================================================
FOUNDER ORDER: realtime voice for Spark clients, first class / top of the line, price rise
  acceptable later if needed. Founder's own refinement, adopted: cap a session and roll
  automatically into a new one so the customer is never shut down.

WHY IT MATTERS (the argument, not the feature): product quality is capped by how well Spark
  understands ONE sentence. "I want to start a bakery" and "a bakery for people with celiac
  who've been left out their whole lives" produce different brands. Today the site gets the
  first. A conversation gets the second. This improves the OUTPUT, not just the experience.

THE ECONOMICS, MEASURED NOT GUESSED (rates confirmed live, July 2026):
  gpt-realtime-2 = $32/M audio in, $64/M out. 1 min of customer speech ~600 tokens; 1 min of
  model speech ~1,200. A 5-minute intake lands around $0.30-$1.00 against a $99 sale — under
  1%. FLAGSHIP IS AFFORDABLE HERE; no price rise needed for this.
  THE TRAP: the API re-sends the ENTIRE conversation every turn, so cost grows with the

  session drops the accumulated context to zero, so cost stays FLAT instead of compounding.

WHAT WAS BUILT:

    OpenAI-Safety-Identifier. Enforces REALTIME_MAX_LEGS (default 8) SERVER-SIDE, so a

    at a pause (waits for response.done; never cuts across a sentence). The microphone stream

  js/spark-voice-ui.js + css/spark-voice.css — the panel: live transcript of both sides
